Just A Zazen Timer is a minimalist, distraction-free meditation timer crafted specifically for Zazen (Zen sitting meditation) and quiet mindfulness practice.

Designed to get out of your way, it gives you everything you need for undisturbed meditation without unnecessary features, subscriptions, or clutter.

Features:
• Minimalist Zen Design: Clean Material 3 interface that respects your focus, supporting both light and dark themes.
• Tibetan Singing Bowl: Authentic Tibetan bowl sound with three mindful dings spaced two seconds apart at the start and end of each session.
• Silent Mode Bypass: Audio is routed through the Alarm stream, ensuring the bells ring reliably even if your phone ringer is switched to Silent or Vibrate.
• Do Not Disturb Integration: Automatically mutes incoming calls and notification interruptions when your sitting begins, and restores your standard settings when the timer finishes.
• Accurate Background Wakeup: Uses exact alarms and wakelock management to ensure reliable timing even when the screen is off or the phone is in deep sleep.
• Quick Sitting Presets: Convenient 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, and 45-minute presets, plus a gentle 10-second preparation period to settle into your posture.
• 100% Free & Open Source: No advertisements, no analytics, no tracking, and no internet connection required. Licensed under the GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPLv3).
